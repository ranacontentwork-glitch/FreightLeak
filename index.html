Questions

Frequently Asked

What exactly does "lifetime license" mean?
You pay once — that's it. You get the app forever on the number of activations you purchased. The seller may ship updates (rules, data files, or fixes) from time to time; those aren’t a guaranteed schedule. No subscription, no renewal, no "lifetime means 5 years" fine print in our license.
Does my invoice data get sent to your servers?
No. FreightLeak runs 100% on your local machine. The only network traffic is to the license server (activate, validate, deactivate) — not your invoice. Your CSV data is never uploaded, stored, or seen by anyone — including us.
What carriers are supported?
FedEx, UPS, and DHL invoice CSVs are auto-detected. If your carrier uses a different format, the built-in column mapper lets you manually map fields in about 3 minutes — and it saves the template for next time.
How does the free demo work?
Download the app and run it without a license key. It processes your real invoice and shows the total recoverable amount — but tracking numbers are blurred and the PDF is watermarked. You see your real dollar figure before you pay a cent.
How is this different from 71lbs, Share a Refund, etc.?
Those services charge 50% of every refund they recover — forever. On a $50K/month shipper, that's $9,000/year. FreightLeak costs $1,497 once, and you keep 100% of every refund from day one. Plus your data never touches a third-party server.
What if I switch laptops?
Use the in-app "Deactivate this machine" button to free up your activation slot, then activate on your new machine. Business tier gives you 2 machines, Enterprise gives you 5.
Do you auto-file disputes with the carrier?
Not yet — and intentionally. Filing disputes involves account-specific authorizations we don't want to complicate. Instead, we generate a professional dispute letter with all tracking numbers and charge codes. You forward it to your carrier rep. Most disputes resolve in 14–30 days.
